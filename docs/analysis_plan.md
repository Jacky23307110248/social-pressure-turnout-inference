# Analysis plan

The primary comparison is assignment to the Neighbors mailer versus control. The outcome is verified turnout in the August 2006 Michigan primary. Treatment was assigned by household within randomization blocks, so voter-level uncertainty is clustered by household.

The primary estimator is the voter-level difference in means. The report uses a 99 percent confidence interval. Supporting analyses include randomization-block and baseline adjustment, household-level aggregation, block-stratified randomization inference, exact-duplicate removal, baseline balance, and leave-one-block-out influence analysis.

The four control-relative treatment comparisons and each declared family of secondary tests use Holm adjustment. Subgroup claims are based on treatment-by-subgroup interactions rather than on comparisons of separate significance levels.

The target claim is a household-assignment policy intention-to-treat effect in the experimental population and allocation environment. Adjacent treatment-arm contrasts compare complete message bundles and are not mediation effects. Spillover dose-response is not estimated because the observed design does not provide adequate within-block-size saturation variation.
